Hi Danny,

I'm Nathan Bates, a new member. I signed up on October 21 as a temporary journeyman through Access Limited. I wanted to share an idea that came out of my sign-up.

When the local reached out to start my registration, I was surprised to learn I had to come into the office in person to do it. When I got there, the only thing required was filling out a packet of forms with a pen: the same name, address and Social Security number written out again on page after page. It made me think the in-person trip and the paper could both be optional.

Moving the packet online could help the local in a few ways:

- **Reach more people.** New members, especially ones working out of town, could sign up from the jobsite instead of taking time off to come in.
- **Fill it out once.** Members enter their information one time and it fills every form in the packet.
- **Cleaner, more organized records.** Typed forms come in complete and readable, every signature is dated and stored, and nothing has to be re-entered from handwriting or filed by hand.
- **It fits with what you already have.** Members already use MTPweb for dues. This would cover the step before someone has an MTPweb account.

To show what I mean, I built a working demo of the new-member packet: the application, the dues checkoff, the journeyman designation and the PAC form, with one e-signature and the initiation fee payment at the end. It uses no real data and doesn't process any money:
https://69af8ad2.union-forms.pages.dev

I also built a second tool, a **logbook for members**. It started as an app I made to track my own hours, and I adapted it for laborers:

- **Hours, signed off on the spot.** Members log their hours each day, and the foreman confirms them by text.
- **Health coverage tracker.** It shows how close a member is to the 300 hours they need, and how long their hour bank will last.
- **Missing-hours check.** It flags months where the trust fund shows fewer hours than the member worked, so a missing contribution gets caught early.
- **Reminders** for out-of-work list re-registration, dues and expiring certs.
- **Apprenticeship progress.** For apprentices, it shows their progress toward the next pay step.

It's the member's own backup record, not a replacement for the hall's or the fund's records.
